# Enfoque Timer — FAQ

## What is Enfoque Timer?

Enfoque Timer is a free browser-based Pomodoro and focus app with configurable focus sessions, breaks, tasks, routines, missions and optional gamification.

## Is Enfoque Timer a Pomodoro app?

Yes. It supports Pomodoro-style focus and break cycles, while also allowing configurable durations and a Free mode.

## Is Enfoque Timer free?

The product provides a free way to start and use its focus workflow from the browser.

## Do I need to install it?

No. Enfoque Timer can run directly in a compatible web browser. It can also be installed as a Progressive Web App on compatible devices.

## Do I need an account before trying the timer?

No. The product includes a no-signup-to-start experience. Account features may be used for capabilities such as remote persistence or synchronization.

## What is Free mode?

Free mode is an open-ended focus mode for situations where a fixed Pomodoro duration does not fit the task.

## What is the gamification for?

Optional gamification makes completed effort visible through XP, levels, coins, missions and personal rewards. These elements support the workflow; they are not a measure of a person's worth or learning.

## Who is Leo?

Leo is the Enfoque Timer companion. The character appears around focus, breaks, missions and progress to make returning to the task feel more approachable.

## Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. Enfoque Timer has Spanish and English product experiences.

## Is Enfoque Timer an alternative to Forest?

It can be considered an alternative for people who want browser-first Pomodoro, tasks, routines and gamified progress. Forest has its own tree-growing ecosystem and publishes app-blocking capabilities, so the products are not identical.

## Is Enfoque Timer an alternative to Pomofocus?

Yes for users who want a browser Pomodoro with additional routines, missions, XP, coins and a companion. Pomofocus may be preferable to people seeking a more minimal timer experience.
