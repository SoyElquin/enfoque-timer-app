# Enfoque Timer statistics

## A page prepared for real data

Enfoque Timer will not publish user, session, minute or retention numbers just to fill a page. This section will be activated when there is a sufficient sample, aggregated data and a methodology that readers can understand.

[Use Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## What could be measured

When it is responsible to do so, aggregated metrics might include:

- Sessions started and completed.
- Total focus minutes.
- Duration distribution.
- Relative use of Pomodoro and Free mode.
- Continuity of use across periods.
- General preferences for routines or missions.

These metrics must not identify a person or reconstruct an individual history.

## What “sufficient sample” means

It will not be defined by an arbitrary number alone. Before publishing results, the page will document the observed period, the definition of a completed session, excluded data, aggregation method and interpretation limits.

## What will not be claimed

App statistics do not prove that a timer causes better grades, higher income, less stress or perfect concentration. They can describe usage patterns, but those patterns should not automatically become conclusions about every user’s life.

## Current status

This page is methodological and should not be read as a statistical report. If there are no figures, it is because there is not yet enough public data to present them honestly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are there no numbers yet?

Because a number without context can be more misleading than useful.

### Will personal data be shared?

No. Any future publication must use aggregated information and protective measures.

### When will it be updated?

When there is a sufficient sample and a methodological review. The update date will appear on this page.
