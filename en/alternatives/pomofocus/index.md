# Pomofocus alternative: Enfoque Timer or Pomofocus

## Quick answer

Pomofocus is a web Pomodoro timer with tasks, templates, finish-time estimates, reports and customizable settings. Enfoque Timer adds missions, routines, experience, levels, coins and personal rewards.

[Explore Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## The main difference

Pomofocus focuses on organizing a day around a Pomodoro timer and a work list. Its official page highlights features such as estimating task time, saving templates, viewing reports and customizing durations, sounds and backgrounds.

Enfoque Timer focuses on connecting the timer with a sense of progress. Alongside focus and breaks, it offers routines, missions and a video-game-inspired motivation layer with Leo.

| If you are looking for… | It may fit better |
|---|---|
| A direct web timer with reports and templates | Pomofocus |
| Missions, experience, levels and coins | Enfoque Timer |
| A minimal functional workspace | Pomofocus |
| A visual gamified experience | Enfoque Timer |
| Custom times, sounds and backgrounds | Pomofocus, depending on the available feature |

## Before you choose

Choose Pomofocus if you want a web tool centered on planning and measuring sessions. Choose Enfoque Timer if a journey of missions, rewards and personal progress helps you return to the work.

Any service can change its features and conditions. Check [Pomofocus](https://pomofocus.io/) and its [web app](https://pomofocus.io/app) before deciding.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Enfoque Timer replace every Pomofocus feature?

Do not assume full equivalence. They are products with different priorities and exact availability depends on the version.

### Which one is simpler?

Pomofocus may feel more direct if you only want a timer and planning. Enfoque Timer adds more progress and game elements.

### Which one works for studying?

Both can support study sessions. The difference is how you prefer to relate to progress.

### Where can I try Enfoque Timer?

At [enfoquetimer.vercel.app](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en).
