# Pomodoro for students

## Quick answer

A Pomodoro timer can help a student turn “I have to study” into a concrete block: choose a topic, focus for a reasonable amount of time, take a break and return.

[Try Enfoque Timer for studying](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## Make a study session easier to start

Before starting, define a small and observable intention. Instead of “study mathematics”, try “solve five exercises” or “review the chapter about functions”. Then start a session and let the block set the limit.

When it ends, take the break. If the topic needs more time, return with a new intention. You do not need to carry the whole study day in your head at once.

## How to use it for studying

1. Choose the topic or mission for the moment.
2. Start a focus block.
3. Keep only the material needed for that block nearby.
4. Take a break when the session ends.
5. Notice what moved forward and decide whether to continue or stop.

Enfoque Timer adds routines and progress so studying does not depend only on the motivation you have that day. The game layer — experience, levels, coins and rewards — can make consistency visible without promising better grades.

## For exams and long assignments

Break the work into stages: understand, practise, review and prepare the submission. Each stage can become a different mission. If a session feels too demanding, try [Free mode](../free-mode/) and choose a more realistic rhythm.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many minutes should a student study?

There is no single duration that works for everyone. Start with a block you can complete and adjust it to the task and your energy.

### Does Pomodoro replace a study plan?

No. It is a tool for executing blocks in a plan, not a replacement for content, practice or academic guidance.

### Can I use it for reading or homework?

Yes. Any activity that can be expressed as a concrete intention can be supported by a session.

### Do I have to study all day?

No. The goal is to work with intention and respect breaks, not to fill every minute with obligations.
