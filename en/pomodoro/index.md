# Pomodoro app: what it is and how to use one

## Quick answer

A Pomodoro app divides work or study into focused sessions and planned breaks. Enfoque Timer brings that idea to the browser and connects it with routines, missions and visible progress.

[Start a session in Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## How it works

1. Choose a concrete intention: study a topic, edit a video, read or move a task forward.
2. Select a focus session.
3. Work until the block ends.
4. Take a break before starting again.
5. Look at your progress and choose the next step.

The method does not require every person to work in exactly 25-minute blocks. You can adapt the duration to your energy, the task and the time of day.

## Why use Enfoque Timer

Enfoque Timer is for people who want something clearer than a stopwatch but lighter than a full project-management platform. You can use a traditional Pomodoro flow or switch to Free mode when you need a different rhythm.

The experience adds a game layer: sessions can become experience, levels, coins and personal rewards. Leo is the visual companion for focus, rest and returning.

## Who is it for?

- Students who need help starting instead of planning forever.
- People who work from home or in front of a screen.
- Creators, designers and developers who work in blocks.
- Anyone who wants to see consistency without turning it into a competition.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Pomodoro only for studying?

No. It can also support writing, coding, design, reading, administrative work and household tasks.

### Do I have to use 25 minutes?

Not necessarily. The classic cycle is a starting point, not a universal rule.

### Does taking a break mean wasting time?

No. A planned break is part of the cycle and helps you decide when to continue.

### Where can I try it?

Open [Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en) in your browser.
