# Pomodoro without signup

## Quick answer

Enfoque Timer is designed so you can understand the experience and start a session without making account creation the first obstacle.

[Open Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## Start with less friction

When someone only needs to focus for the next few minutes, a form can become the reason they leave. Enfoque Timer therefore prioritizes the action: choose an intention, start the timer and see whether the flow helps.

You can try a Pomodoro session, explore Free mode and learn how focus, breaks and progress fit together. Synchronization, persistence and other features may depend on the current version and user settings.

## What “without signup” means

It means signup should not be the first barrier to trying the timer. It does not mean that every future service or advanced feature will work without an account. If the app asks for access to a specific feature, review what is being enabled before continuing.

## Is it for you?

This approach may fit if you:

- Want to try a Pomodoro before committing to another tool.
- Need to start a one-off session in a browser.
- Prefer exploring first and deciding later whether to save more information.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I try the timer without creating an account?

The experience is designed so signup is not the first step. Exact availability can change by version.

### Does no signup mean that nothing is saved?

Not necessarily. The way information is saved or synchronized depends on the current experience and settings.

### Can I use it for studying?

Yes. Choose a concrete intention and start a browser session.

### What should I do after trying it?

If the flow works for you, explore [routines, missions and progress](../gamified/) to build a more consistent practice.
