# Gamified Pomodoro

## Quick answer

A gamified Pomodoro timer adds progress signals to a focus session. Enfoque Timer uses experience, levels, coins, rewards, routines, missions and its visual companion Leo.

[Try the gamified Pomodoro](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## Focus can become visible

A traditional timer answers one question: “How much time is left?” Enfoque Timer adds another: “What progress am I building?” After a session, a visible consequence can help you choose the next mission.

The game layer is not designed as a penalty. You do not need to lose points for resting or compete with other people. The goal is to give everyday progress a clear and friendly signal.

## How the experience works

1. Choose a mission or intention.
2. Start a focus session.
3. Complete the block and respect the break.
4. Receive progress, experience or coins according to the available experience.
5. Use personal rewards to make consistency visible.

Leo accompanies different moments of the cycle: focus, rest, missions and celebration. The video-game language makes the process more approachable without turning life into another scoreboard.

## Who might like it?

- People motivated by seeing small advances.
- Users who leave when a timer feels too cold.
- Students and creators who want to connect sessions with missions.
- People who prefer positive motivation over penalties.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes a Pomodoro gamified?

It connects time measurement with progress, rewards or a narrative of advancement.

### Does gamification require competition?

No. Enfoque Timer focuses on personal progress and does not need a public leaderboard.

### Can I use it without wanting to “play”?

Yes. The visual layer can remain optional while you use the timer as a focus tool.

### Does the app guarantee that I will be more productive?

No. A tool can structure time, but results depend on the task, context and person.
