# Free mode: a Pomodoro timer at your pace

## Quick answer

Free mode lets you start a focus block without feeling that you must obey a fixed cycle. It helps when you want to choose the duration or when a task does not fit the classic Pomodoro rhythm.

[Try Free mode](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## When to use it

Free mode may help when:

- A task needs a short, specific block.
- You are starting and 25 minutes feels too demanding.
- You want deeper work without changing phases frequently.
- You are doing creative work and want to protect your rhythm.
- You want to observe how long you can dedicate without a rigid target.

Flexibility does not mean working without stopping. Decide when the block ends and plan a break afterwards. Rest remains part of the system.

## Pomodoro mode or Free mode

Pomodoro mode offers a repeatable structure of focus and breaks. Free mode gives you control over the rhythm. Neither is best for everyone; the task and the moment matter.

A practical approach is to use Free mode to overcome the resistance to starting and then switch to structured sessions when you need continuity.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Free mode remove breaks?

It should not be used to ignore rest. Its purpose is to adapt the block, not to turn continuous work into an obligation.

### Is it useful for studying?

Yes. Use it for reading, practice or long assignments when you want to choose the block yourself.

### Is it just a stopwatch?

Not exactly. It remains part of Enfoque Timer’s focus experience, progress and context.

### Can I return to classic Pomodoro?

Yes. Choose the format that fits your next intention.
