# Free Pomodoro

## Quick answer

Enfoque Timer lets you start a Pomodoro session in a browser without making a purchase an obstacle to trying the experience.

[Start free with Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## What you can do

You can start focus sessions, configure breaks, try Free mode, create routines and missions and observe your progress. The experience also includes game elements such as experience, levels, coins and personal rewards.

This is useful if you want to see whether a focus timer fits you before adopting a more complex system. Open the app, choose an intention and begin with a small block.

## Free does not mean forcing yourself

A free tool does not need to turn every session into a performance test. Enfoque Timer is built around a sustainable cycle: focus, rest and return. If a session does not go as expected, adjust the next block.

Features, platforms and conditions can change over time. Check the official experience before making decisions about plans or payments.

## Who is it for?

- People looking for an online Pomodoro timer.
- Students who want to start quickly.
- Workers who need to separate work into blocks.
- Users who prefer trying an app before signing up or paying.
