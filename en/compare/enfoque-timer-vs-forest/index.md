# Enfoque Timer vs Forest: an honest comparison in 2026

## Quick answer

Forest turns focus into the care of a virtual tree. Enfoque Timer turns focus into sessions, breaks, missions and gamified progress. Forest may fit better if protecting a plant motivates you; Enfoque Timer may fit better if you prefer a video-game experience with Leo, levels, coins and rewards.

[Try Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)

## What the sources say

The [official Forest site](https://forestapp.cc/) describes planting and growing a tree during focus. The [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forest-focus-for-productivity/id866450515) shows additional features that may depend on platform.

This page describes Enfoque Timer according to its public experience and does not attempt to list every Forest feature. Check official sources for current prices, availability and policies.

## Verdict by user type

- Choose Forest if the tree metaphor helps you stay in the session.
- Choose Enfoque Timer if missions, levels, coins and a visual companion motivate you.
- Try both if you do not know whether an ecological consequence or a video-game narrative works better for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Enfoque Timer better than Forest?

Not for everyone. The choice depends on your motivation and the structure you want.

### Do Forest and Enfoque Timer use the same technique?

Both may use focus sessions inspired by Pomodoro, but their product experiences and metaphors are different.

### Which one is kinder to rest?

Enfoque Timer communicates rest as part of progress. In Forest, some mechanics make the consequence of leaving a session more visible.

At [enfoquetimer.vercel.app](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en).
