# Sources and editorial criteria

Updated: August 2026

Comparison pages use public information and prioritize official sources:

- [Enfoque Timer](https://enfoquetimer.vercel.app/en)
- [Forest](https://forestapp.cc/)
- [Forest on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forest-focus-for-productivity/id866450515)
- [Pomofocus](https://pomofocus.io/)
- [Pomofocus app](https://pomofocus.io/app)

These comparisons are not paid rankings. Each product is described according to its main proposition and the type of person it may serve best.

## Editorial rules

- Do not invent numbers, reviews or features.
- Do not present one universal winner.
- Do not claim that an app guarantees better grades, productivity or health outcomes.
- Do not compare prices without a date, country and plan.
- Mark features that depend on platform, version or settings.
- Check official product pages before updating a comparison.

The Enfoque Timer statistics page remains a methodology proposal until enough aggregated data exists. Numbers will not be published just to create authority.
